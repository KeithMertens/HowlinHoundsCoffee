About Howlin' Hounds

A Historic Brick Coffee Haven in Omaha's Old Market

Tucked away on South 16th Street in downtown Omaha, Howlin' Hounds Coffee occupies a stunning historic building featuring exposed brick walls, gleaming hardwood floors, and classic tin ceilings.

Known for exceptional latte art, inventive house-made syrups (like cinnamon roll), decadent cold foam, and premium specialty roasts, we offer a speakeasy-like ambiance that invites you to slow down, connect with neighbors, and savor every sip.

Specialty Espresso Bar

Carefully sourced beans, silky cortados, and velvety lattes.

Historic Rustic Charm

Exposed brick, tin ceilings, and cozy soda-fountain bar seating.
